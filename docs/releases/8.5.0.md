## MTG Tracker & Analytics v8.5.0

Release focused on making the statistics correct and the app easier to use on a tablet.

### Main changes

- **Draws are neutral in the win rate.** A drawn match no longer counts as a loss. The rate is now computed over decisive games only, and the draw stays visible as its own number (`6W-3L-1P`). Season archives that were snapshotted under the old denominator are rebuilt automatically, so past standings match the new rule.
- **Larger tap targets on iPad.** Compact controls such as filter chips were drawn below the 44pt platform minimum and needed a precise press. They now borrow the missing touch area without changing their appearance.
- **Previous-season rankings** and season top-ten standings in the Arena pages.
- Fixes to the administrator Operations page, and alignment of the backup and health-alert configuration with the real VM setup.

### Compatibility

No forced update. The accompanying database migration is additive: existing 8.4 clients keep working and simply ignore the new field.
