## 21Life v9.0.0

21Life is the new name of the app. Your account, decks, playgroups and match
history remain exactly where they were.

### Highlights

- **A refined table experience.** The game wizard has clearer seat previews,
  more readable table layouts and larger controls during play.
- **Better reports.** Deck and match details turn key results into visual
  summaries instead of long lists of numbers.
- **Player Awards.** Discover the top three players for ten achievements, from
  Veteran to King of the Arena. Deck and player awards are now separate tabs.
- **Smarter notifications.** Participants are notified when a match is saved,
  alongside improved playgroup-invite and season-end messages.
- **A more useful profile and home.** Personal statistics, deck insights and
  playgroup cards are easier to scan at a glance.
- **A consistent dark interface.** The web and Android apps share the approved
  neutral-black and neon-green visual system, with improved contrast and touch
  targets.
- **Safer sign-in.** Android and web login fields now work better with password
  managers and do not automatically capitalize passwords.

### Compatibility

No action is required. Version 9 keeps compatibility with supported 8.1+ app
clients and uses additive database changes only.
